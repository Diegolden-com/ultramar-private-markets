## Tips for recording

- **Cursor**: Move slowly and deliberately. Pause for 1-2 seconds on each section before narrating.
- **Simulator**: Click "Approved" first, let the result fully render, then click "Generic Router" and let the revert render. Don't click through too fast.
- **Audio**: Record voice separately if possible (voiceover is cleaner than live narration).
- **Resolution**: Record at 1080p so the terminal commands and SVG charts are legible.
- **No cursor drifting**: Don't wave the cursor during static sections. On camera it's distracting.
